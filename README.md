### 🚀 Featured Projects

**🏦 RegBrain — Self-Verifying RAG for RBI Compliance**
A retrieval-augmented Q&A system over Reserve Bank of India regulations that refuses to guess — every generated claim runs through a dual-gate verifier (lexical overlap + neural cross-encoder scoring) before it reaches the user, and abstains rather than hallucinate when the evidence is thin. Benchmarked against a naive RAG baseline: **0% hallucination rate vs 2.9%**, and 4.04 verified citations per answer vs 1.71.
`Python` `FastAPI` `Qdrant` `Next.js` `Groq`
[Live Demo](https://reg-brain.vercel.app) · [Code](https://github.com/abhijithbhat/RegBrain)

**🏟️ AegisGrid 2026 — Incident-Fusion AI Copilot**
An explainable decision-support copilot for stadium safety supervisors, built for Google's Hack2Skill PromptWars. Deterministic code owns the risk scoring and routing math; Gemini only interprets unstructured reports and flags contradictions — every recommendation stays human-approved and fully auditable. Backed by 107 automated tests at 93%+ coverage and a live Cloud Run deployment.
`Next.js` `Firestore` `Cloud Run` `Gemini`
[Live Demo](https://aegisgrid-2026-799660927467.us-central1.run.app) · [Code](https://github.com/abhijithbhat/aegisgrid-2026)

**⚖️ FairHire Dharma — AI Bias Detection**
Detects and explains bias in hiring algorithms, built for Google's Hack to Scale.
`SHAP` `Fairlearn` `spaCy`
[Code](https://github.com/abhijithbhat/fairhire-dharma)

**🛡️ ContentGuard — AI Safety Benchmark**
An OpenEnv environment that teaches AI agents that *which* mistake they make matters, not just whether they make one — asymmetric severity-weighted rewards, a prompt-injection resistance task, and a live multi-model leaderboard. Built for the Meta × HuggingFace OpenEnv Hackathon.
`FastAPI` `Docker` `HuggingFace Spaces` `OpenEnv v1.0`
[Live Demo](https://abhijithbhat-openenv-project.hf.space) · [Code](https://github.com/abhijithbhat/openenv-project)
